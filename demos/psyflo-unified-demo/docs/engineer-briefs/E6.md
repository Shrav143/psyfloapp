Current master: `FamilyLink`, `ParentSign`, `ParentPortal`, `ParentEnroll`, `ParentProgress`, `ParentQuarterly`, `ParentLetters`, `ParentSchedule`, `ParentSurvey`, `SchoolPortal`, `ReferralForm`, `ReferralSent`, `AppealLetter`, actions `x-family.js`, `x-portals.js`, `x-school.js`, `x-quarterly.js`.

Build:
1. **Family portal** (390 px phone, token links `/f/<token>`, `/parent/<token>/<Screen>`; routes stay): checklist home "3 of 7 done" (Confirm contact details · Provide insurance information · Complete required paperwork · Review and sign where permitted · Schedule or confirm an appointment · Review progress updates · Complete the check-in · View insurance letters), one task per screen, body 16 px, semantic `h1/h2` and landmarks, language toggle ≥44 px at the top (English/Español everywhere it exists today), targets ≥44 px, plain words, no clinical terms. **Signature screen follows the signature-mode setting** from Settings (Remote allowed · In person required · Pending · Completed · Ready to review) with a "Type your name" alternative; in person-required mode it shows what to bring and when; never implies a digital signature replaces a required in-person one; consent recording per rules. Letters: What this says · What you can do · By when (with the date's source). Progress, quarterly, schedule, survey carry the maturity chip "Pilot (preview)/Future" per `maturity.js` only in the presenter overlay. Guardian sees only their child's plain-language material; no clinical narrative.
2. **Appeal (guardian side, `AppealLetter`)**: guardian reviews, edits wording, signs or calls; the letter is prepared by staff in Denial Intelligence (E3); Psyflo never exercises appeal rights; states Draft · Ready to review · Signed · Submitted by staff.
3. **School portal** (desktop, school login): home with one "Harvest needs" card (missing referral information) above the referral list; referral form in four sections with required first (49 inputs today → grouped); submit referral, complete missing information, authorized classroom observations, feedback, status text only; never clinical content, other schools' students, or outpatient information; permitted updates are non-clinical (credited to the counselor).
4. Data capture reuse: what the family or school gave is carried forward to the referral/eligibility (E2) and screening (E5) without re-entry.
5. Accessibility pass on every screen you touch (headings, landmarks, labels, contrast, focus, errors announced) and 390 px / 1024 px layouts with no horizontal scroll; screenshots (look at them).
6. Notes `seed/notes/e6.md`; tests `test/family.mjs`, `test/school.mjs` (disclosure limits, signature modes, language toggle, token access only to own child); flowD (family link + eligibility) still passes.

Done when: S2's family step runs (link sent → five steps → eligibility "details received"), signature mode switches change the screens, the school sees only permitted data, smoke clean for all portal routes, a11y checks pass.
