## 4. Contradictions and open questions

### 4.1 Remote versus in-person guardian signatures

- **For in person:** P-016 (face to face intake, wet signatures, clients 14+ also consent); P-023 (parent signs each form even when pre-filled); P-015; P-019 and P-020 (capacity and family barriers).
- **For remote:** P-021 and P-022 (enrollment signed before intake, quarterly signed on the phone, virtual intake or reassessment where allowed); P-077 (guardian appeal letter signed by link); P-024 (signature tracking).
- **Why it is unresolved:** P-017. The only person asked deferred to the Director of Programs, and no source records the Director's answer. The leadership enthusiasm on 4 Nov is a wish, not a statement that the rule permits remote signature; its own qualifier is where allowed.
- **Outside the recordings:** WM reports that the DMAS SRA form itself says actual or electronic signature. That concerns the clinician's attestation, a different document, but it shows Medicaid is not categorically against electronic signature.
