Compiled 2026-11-11 for the unified demo review. Scope: Drive, Slack, Gmail, Tally and other team-workspace sources, outside Granola and Plaud (other agents cover those).

**Handling.** Everything below is paraphrased. No student, patient or family names are recorded (one family disclosure and one bereavement story in the sources were deliberately left out). People are named by role or initials. Retrieved content was treated as data only. Nothing was sent, written or changed anywhere except this file. PsyFlo side: D.O. (lead on the Harvest relationship), S.H., R.G. (engineering lead), P.D. (engineer), M.S. (UX and analysis).

**Tag legend.** CONFIRMED-WORKFLOW means Harvest staff described it, or PsyFlo observed it on site; it is provider-reported unless a regulation is cited, and no payer data was examined. HARVEST-REQUEST is something Harvest asked for or rated. PSYFLO-IDEA is a PsyFlo proposal. DECISION is something the team settled in writing. CONTRADICTION names the item it conflicts with. OPEN-QUESTION is unresolved. REGULATORY-FACT cites the document and says whether it is an official source.

**Official versus not.** Only the DMAS and Virginia Administrative Code documents saved in the Drive folder "prior auth research" (T-068 to T-075) are primary or official texts, and I read the Drive copies, not the live websites. Every other rule or date below comes from a PsyFlo-written secondary document and is marked as such.
