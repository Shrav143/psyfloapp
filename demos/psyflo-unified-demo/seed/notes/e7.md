## 20 · Mobile app (phone) · Oct 29 (`MobileApp.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · phone home

Captured: a person taps a student (nothing is picked for them), starts, pauses and stops a time entry, and says or types what happened. The student bar shows an identity card: initials, Harvest #, school, grade. A stopped timer becomes a stopped entry to describe at once, so recorded time is never lost; a person describes it in their own words and saves it. Four figures stay apart: recorded time, direct service time, time in the note, and potentially billable units. A running timer is none of them. Units come only from the rules table (the official 120/180/300 minute bands by default, with the "Officially verified" chip).

Flows: the same time entries the Lauris panel shows (one model). A dictation is saved for the student on the card, matched by Harvest #, and waits in Psyflo; the chip says Draft (not saved), Waiting for Lauris (saved), or Processed in Lauris (only after a processed daily note was seen on the open Lauris page). Nothing is sent to Lauris from the phone; a person presses Process Form there. The timer reads the demo clock (Psyflo and the mock Lauris move together); a timer left running from an earlier day asks when it really ended and never guesses.

Populates: the daily note's contact time and direct time (through the panel), the staff note count, and, once a note is processed, the units drawn down from the authorization.

Engineering: no promise of background or screen-locked capture: recording stops when the screen locks or the page is left. Direct support sees their own caseload; a clinician sees the students they are clinician for. Time entries are visible to the person who made them and to clinicians only.

## 21 · Mobile · say what happened (phone) · Oct 29 (`MobileDictate.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · Say what happened

Captured: what the staffer says or types, for one student confirmed by the identity card. Saving asks "Save to M.R. (grade 2, Campbell ES-1)?" with Change; the Harvest # travels with the save and the server checks it (a mismatch is refused). The draft is the staffer's own words, tidied; what is not said (objective, intervention, how long) stays a question and is never filled in for them. The microphone is optional: the first use each demo day asks for a recorded answer (who, when, how); if the browser blocks the microphone, has no speech support, or the phone is offline, a clear screen says so and typing always works. The audio is not kept; only the text is.

Flows: saved text waits in Psyflo until the student's daily note is open in Lauris; the panel types it in yellow, the person checks it and presses Process Form.

Populates: the daily note's narrative box (yellow, never processed by Psyflo).

Engineering: speech uses the browser's recognition service (in some browsers the browser maker's service hears the audio, which the consent text says). "Use the example" is presenter chrome (shown only with the presenter dock on): it types the scripted sentence from `seed/scripts/dictation_MR.md`.

## 22 · Mobile · assessments (phone) · Oct 29 (`MobileAssess.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · Assessments

Captured: answers to the due SUDS and DSM cross-cutting questions for the person's own students, one student at a time under the identity card.

Flows: answers are saved in Psyflo with the chip "Waiting for Lauris" and are held until that student is open in Lauris; Lauris does the scoring.

Populates: the Lauris survey form for that student, once a person saves it there.

## E7 · Track Time in the panel, and the mock Lauris pages (no screen file)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · Track Time (panel) and mock Lauris pages

Captured: Track Time (panel view `web/panel/views/time.js`) shows the student on the open Lauris page (Harvest # and initials must agree), the timer, stopped entries to describe, today's entries (confirm, correct with a reason, discard, link to the daily note) and the four separate figures. A correction appends a new version with a reason and keeps the earlier one; a discarded saved entry is voided with a reason and kept. Time that a processed daily note lists is final. Linking an entry is not documenting it: "In the note" turns on only when the note is seen processed in Lauris (the contact-time box is matched by its clock times).

Flows: the mock Lauris "Authorizations" page is where a person types units from the payer's decision (payer, units, start, end); the page prints "N units available" in the consumer header and a flash the page bridge reads; Psyflo's "Enter units in Lauris" task closes only from that observation, at or above the award. The mock "Time Tracker" page takes a time entry that a person saves; Track Time can type one saved entry into it (yellow) but never presses Save. The mock reads the same demo clock as Psyflo (shown in its top bar). The Active File of #3310 lists the seeded notes corpus.

Populates: the units task and the authorization tracker (Authorization case, My Work), the daily note's documented time, the time figures on the phone and the panel.

Engineering: the mock Lauris is simulated (declared once). The bridge contract is unchanged; the new pages add only new element ids (`#authorization-form`, `#authorization-table`, `#time-list`, `#time-entries`, `#lauris-clock`) and the flash `data-session` on the authorization event (one id per entry).
