## 0 · Sign in (`Login.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · Sign in

Captured: who is signing in, chosen from the roster of staff (names and roles only). Four role cards (Direct support, Supervisor, Clinician, Admin); a role with several people lets you pick the person. The family portal and the school counselor have their own entries because they have no staff login.

Flows: the sign-in creates a server-side session; every call after it carries that session and the server decides what the person may see and do. The first screen is the role's home (My Work).

Engineering: demo-grade identity. There is no password and no identity provider; the server enforces the permission table all the same. Nothing pre-fills a person.

## 1 · Where things live · read first (`WhereThingsLive.dc.html`)

HOW TO READ THE DEMO · Where things live

The rule on this screen is the one the demo follows: a Lauris form is written by the extension on the page a person has open (screening, intake, plan, notes, both SRAs); everything that is not a Lauris form lives in the Psyflo app (My Work, referrals, send and track, payer replies, partial decisions, the peer-to-peer brief, analytics, settings); guardians and schools use portals. There is no Lauris API: Psyflo reads and types only on the open page, through a page bridge.

Captured: nothing. The same content, with the architecture, the maturity legend and the unresolved decisions, is in Settings, Presenter tab.

## 51 · My Work (`MyWork.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · My Work

Captured: nothing new. One list of what needs the signed-in person, built from stored referrals, notes, requests, clocks and reminders. Every line is computed: nothing is typed in and nothing is invented. Dates come from the demo clock and every number that is a rule (decision follow-up day, note deadline, units per day) reads the rules table and shows its source chip.

Flows: the sections depend on the role. Direct support: My day (progress, next note, due next, to fix). Supervisor: Review, Team, To do. Clinician: To do, Review, Team, Referrals. Admin: Operations and Team, status, units and deadlines only, with "Clinical text hidden for your role". Today shows the top three with a verb ("Send reply", "Review draft", "Approve"); Show all, This week and Waiting on others open on demand. A line whose screen the role may not open says why. Approve, Return and "That didn't happen" call note.review (the reviewer is never the author); Require approval and Turn off call staff.reviewMode; a person with the right to confirm sees a designation or a role raise proposed by someone else and confirms it here.

Populates: the note's review trail, the staff record's approval mode, the audit log.

Engineering: pure selectors in web/js/shared/sel/mywork.js (importable by the server and the tests). Notes behind and on time are counted from the notes the extension reports at Process Form: a daily note is due notes.daily_due_hours (Harvest target, 24) after the session ends; a session with no Psyflo timer ends at the 3:30 pm dismissal. The weekly guardian call is due by Friday, the monthly collateral by month end. "Units low" uses the run-out rate (an unverified assumption, said on the line) and the continued-stay lead time. The old Do next, Review queue and Staff dashboard routes open this screen; add ?legacy=1 to reach the old pages.

## 55 · Settings (`Settings.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · Settings

Captured: an administrator's changes, each with a reason: people and roles, designated access, rules, unit bands, signature mode per document type, family-link questions, recording consent. Nothing here is imported.

Flows: People (add a user; change a role: lowering applies at once, raising waits for a second person). Roles (the permission matrix, drawn from the same table the server uses; designated access proposed by an administrator and confirmed by an administrator or clinician who did not propose it; nobody designates themselves). Units (the daily unit bands with the official default and the alternate; deadlines and clocks). Rules (every rule with its value, source category and verification date; edit, reset to the default, history). Audit (append-only events with actor, kind, simulated flag, filters and a CSV download). Family link (signature mode per document: Remote allowed or In person required, default Remote allowed with the chip Policy not validated; the questions the family link asks; link expiry; recording consent). Presenter (how it fits, architecture, the maturity legend and the unresolved decisions with where each is set).

Populates: the rules table, the staff records, state.designations and state.role_changes, the family link questions the guardian pages read, the audit log. A change applies from now on: stored dates and counts are not recomputed.

Engineering: actions settings.role, settings.roleDecide, settings.userAdd, settings.designate, settings.designateDecide, settings.familyQuestion (server/psyflo/actions/x-settings.js) and the foundation's settings.rule. Administrators have no clinical-record access, and a designation cannot grant it.

## 27 · Supervisor review queue (Psyflo app) (`ReviewQueue.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · Supervisor review queue

Captured: the reviewer's decision: Approve, Return with a note, or That didn't happen. This route now opens My Work (Review); "Who needs approval" is the Team tab.
