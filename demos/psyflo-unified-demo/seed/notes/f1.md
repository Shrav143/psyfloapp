## 19 · Sent · a person presses Send (`Submission.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · the request moves only through recorded people and observed page events.

Captured: Process Form, Get Signature and the signer's name come from the page bridge to the open Lauris page (consumer #, session id, Lauris user). The internal approval is recorded by a designated approver. A person with the send capability presses Send; the receipt is the simulated Sfax stub and says so. The decision clock is "Day N" from the rules table (follow-up on day 10 and overdue after 14 are Harvest-reported; the public 7-day standard is shown as a reference only).

Flows: Process Form -> Pending clinical approval -> Approve -> Get Signature (observed) -> Signed -> Send (simulated Sfax or the payer portal, read back from the open portal page) -> Submitted.

## 18 · Extension · sign-off (Forms to Approve) (`ExtSignOff.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · what the panel shows is read from the open page; nothing is signed by Psyflo.

Captured: the form preview is read from the open Lauris page. Psyflo never clicks Process Form, Get Signature or Send. "Signed" is recorded only when the page shows the signature, with the name of the Lauris user who signed. If that user is not an LMHP-type clinician, Lauris refuses and Psyflo records nothing. If Get Signature was pressed before the internal approval was recorded, Psyflo keeps the observed fact on hold and applies it when the approval is recorded.

Flows: panel reads page -> person presses a Lauris button -> page shows the result -> bridge reports it -> Psyflo records it with the Harvest #, session id and user.

## 29 · Authorizations (`Authorizations.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · requests come from drafts, observed Lauris events and recorded payer decisions; units come from the clinic's record of awards.

Captured: a payer decision is recorded with its document reference and cannot be overwritten (a correction adds a version with a reason). An approved decision sets the award in Psyflo's tracker and opens a task: a person enters the units on the Lauris Authorizations page, and the task closes when the open page shows them. Psyflo does not write units into Lauris. Deadlines show their source: Officially verified, Payer-specific, Harvest-reported or Unverified demo assumption.

Flows: decision recorded -> award in the tracker -> task "enter the units in Lauris" -> person enters them -> page bridge sees them -> task closed.

## 42 · Analytics (`Analytics.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · three datasets, never summed.

Captured: case-backed counts come from stored requests and notes. The seeded payer and staff figures are "Sample data, illustrative" (provenance harvest_export): Harvest has not computed them. The v3 cohort is illustrative and generated separately. Groups smaller than the minimum group size (rule analytics.min_group_size, 5) are suppressed.

Flows: stored records -> selectors -> one dataset per view with its banner.
