## 53 · Denial Intelligence (`DenialIntelligence.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · a case opens by itself when a request needs a payer-decision response; nothing is typed to start it.

Captured: the case is derived from the request. A request that enters Partial: decide, Info requested, a peer-to-peer stage, Denied or Appeal sent opens a case (DI-<request number>) with the payer's message, the document reference if one is on file, and the deadline with its source chip (payer-stated, rule-based or "Not verified"). The status, next action and deadline are computed from the request after every action, so this queue, the Authorizations queue and the rail count cannot disagree. The four seeded cases (D.W., K.T., T.N., E.C.) match their requests; their evidence records are synthetic and marked Illustrative sample.

Flows: request stage changes (a person records a payer reply, or the presenter's receive-reply simulation) -> the case opens -> the owner works it in the workspace -> the request leaves the adverse stages -> the case closes with its outcome. Administrators see status, units and deadlines only; clinical text is for clinicians.

## 54 · Denial case · decision, evidence, options, action, history (`DenialCase.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · what the payer said comes from the recorded request; evidence comes from stored dated records; drafts are built only from that evidence.

Captured: Decision shows what the payer communicated, how it arrived (phone, Availity, fax) and the document reference. A decision that arrived by phone has no document until a person records the letter or fax reference; a decision is stored once and a correction needs a reason. Evidence lists dated records from the last 30 days (a Harvest-reported window) with a source chip each; what is not documented prints "Evidence missing — verify ...". Sensitive records (an incident report) stay out of drafts unless a clinician includes them.

Flows: Options lists only the paths that are valid for this case and payer, each with its deadline and a verification chip; reconsideration shows "Payer rule not verified"; the provider path for an appeal shows "Not verified". Action holds the workflow: Decide (accept as offered, stretch, use the units up: logged only, nothing is sent); Information (one answer per item asked, drafted from records, reviewed by a clinician, then a person presses Send: simulated Sfax for fax payers, the payer portal in the clinician's own login otherwise, with the confirmation read from that page); Peer-to-peer (concerns, dated evidence, editable talking points, call date, typed notes, outcome with its document; recording is off unless the rule is switched on); Appeal (a clinician reviews the letter, the guardian reads and signs on the family portal, staff review the signed letter and press Send; guardian-led by default); Reconsideration (draft, review, send, record the answer). An approved outcome sets the award in Psyflo's tracker and opens a task for a person to enter the units in Lauris; Psyflo never writes units into Lauris. Drafts are rule-based and labelled Simulated.

## 34 · Insurer asks for more information · Oct 29 (`InfoRequest.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · the payer's questions are read from the payer message; the reply is drafted from stored records and sent by a person.

Captured: the items the payer asked for, the time the request arrived and the payer's own deadline (the 24-hour rule is only the fallback). The reply is one answer per item, each from dated stored records; anything not documented is marked Evidence missing and must be filled or removed before a clinician reviews it.

Flows: this route now opens the Denial case (Action > Information). Send: simulated Sfax for fax payers (the reference comes from the stub); for Availity or the UHC portal the clinician sends in their own login and the confirmation number is read from the open portal page. A sent reply is write-once and the decision clock keeps its first send date.

## 35 · Partials & denials · Oct 29 (`Partials.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · the payer's offer is recorded with who and when; the choice is logged, not sent.

Captured: the decision on a partial approval, with the document reference when one is on file.

Flows: this route now opens the Denial case (Action > Decide). Accept as offered, accept and stretch, accept and use them up, or ask for a peer-to-peer. All are internal records; an accepted award goes to Psyflo's tracker and a task asks a person to enter the units in Lauris. A denial goes to Appeal (guardian-led by default) or Reconsideration (payer rule not verified).

## 38 · Peer-to-peer call notes · Oct 29 (`PeerBrief.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · the talking points come from stored dated evidence; the call date and the outcome are entered by a person.

Captured: the call date the payer agreed by phone, the payer reference and reviewer if given, typed notes during the call, and the outcome with the document that states it (required for any decision). Recording the call is off unless the rule p2p.recording_enabled is on, and then needs everyone's consent.

Flows: this route now opens the Denial case (Action > Peer-to-peer). Request -> prepare points -> call date -> hold the call -> outcome with its document -> the request becomes Active (units to the tracker, a task for Lauris) or Denied.

## 40 · Payer playbook (`PayerPlaybook.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · seeded sample figures, labelled as such.

Captured: what each payer wants, how to send and its clocks come from the rules table with a source chip each. The per-payer counts are seeded sample data (Sample data, illustrative), not Harvest's figures. The list of rules Psyflo applies to every draft is the state key drafting_rules (read by Settings).

Flows: the content opens as a drawer from the Denial case ("Payer guidance"); Analytics > Payer can open the same drawer.
