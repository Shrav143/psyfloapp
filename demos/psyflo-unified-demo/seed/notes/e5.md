## 13 · Extension · home (`ExtHome.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · the panel reads the Lauris page the person has open and Psyflo's own records. It types nothing on its own authority.

Captured: the Lauris user on the open page becomes the panel's session (a person Psyflo does not know gets no access; an administrator gets "Not permitted", because the administrator role has no clinical-record access). The open student is matched by Harvest # and by the initials on the page; a mismatch is a hard stop with an identity card and "Change student". Three equal buttons: Scribe, Track Time and Scan & Enhance (the third only with the scanEnhance capability, otherwise one line says why). The next task, the "to review" row and the state-portal clock banner are computed from stored referrals, requests, shares and incidents. The demo role toggles are gone: the presenter dock switches roles.

Scan & Enhance (the third button): two modes. Mode A, Review draft, reads the open draft form: findings are questions with the rule and the evidence beside them (a flagged word, a percentage, no objective, no intervention, units that the minutes do not support, a sentence already in recent notes). The person answers in their own words; accepting applies exactly their answer; Compare shows before and after; Approve and fill types only the changed boxes (yellow) and the person presses Process Form. Mode B, Evidence review, is read-only: it reads Psyflo's captured copies, never a Lauris scrape, and offers no fill. It shows documented interventions with dates, goal coverage, gaps ("Evidence missing - verify ..."), repeated wording and the marks (who, when). There is no addendum and no late-entry feature; the server refuses any write to a processed record.

Flows: open a Lauris page -> the page bridge reports it -> the panel picks a view (home, Scribe, Prepare authorization, Scan & Enhance, incident, documents, Track Time) -> a person presses a panel button -> Psyflo types yellow boxes into the open form -> a person presses Process Form, Get Signature or Send in Lauris.

Populates: nothing by itself. What a person does (a draft decision, a fill, a mark) is recorded by the actions named on the other Extension screens.

Engineering: web/panel/core.js (bridge messages, session, router, shared chrome), views/*.js (one module per view, registry in views/index.js), lib/*.js (recorder, consent, review, boxes). The in-page fallback and the MV3 side panel run the same code. The bridge contract (element ids, message shapes) is unchanged. aria-live lives on one status region only.

## 14 · Scribe · screening call (`ExtScreening.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · call. Pilot: consent-gated.

Captured: before the call, boxes the referral and the family link already answer are typed (yellow); nothing else is. Recording starts only after a recorded consent (who agreed, when); "Type instead" records a refusal. While recording, the panel lists only what the insurer still needs, each as a question. When the call ends the draft holds only what was said or is on the referral; the decision and the diagnosis stay with the clinician. The transcript is text from the browser's speech engine (or the scripted example call); no audio is kept.

Flows: consent -> record or play the scripted call -> Stop -> draft with sources -> Fill -> the clinician checks the yellow boxes -> Process Form (observed).

Populates: the screening record (transcript, boxes), the recording-consent record, the 30-day send clock when Process Form is observed.

## 17 · Prepare authorization · request (`ExtSRA.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · every statement is built from stored records (screening, intake, plan, referral, school counts, the request record). Each carries source chips that open the stored row or print "Evidence missing - verify ...". Dates and units come from the request record, never typed by hand. Criterion 3 and trauma-informed care are left for the clinician.

Captured: the three-way review (Staff input, PsyFlo wording, Source) with accept, reject, edit wording and restore previous; there is no accept-all and Approve and fill stays disabled until every box has a decision. Each draft and decision is kept in doc_versions (Original, PsyFlo draft, Clinician edited, Approved; Saved and Processed only from the observed page event).

Flows: draft -> review each box -> Approve and fill (typed, yellow) -> Process Form (observed) -> Pending clinical approval -> Approve -> Get Signature (observed) -> Send.

## 18 · Prepare authorization · sign-off (`ExtSignOff.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · the form preview is read from the open Lauris page. The panel names who approves (staff holding the approve.sra designation), who may sign (LMHP-type users, Officially verified) and who sends, and shows Pending clinical approval until the approver presses Approve (request.approve). Psyflo never signs: "Signed" is recorded only when the page shows the signature, with the name of the Lauris user who signed.

Flows: Process Form (observed) -> Pending clinical approval -> Approve in the panel -> Get Signature in Lauris (observed) -> Signed -> Send in Psyflo.

## 23 · Scribe · daily note (`ExtDPN.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · what the staffer says, or types, in the panel, or a dictation the phone left waiting. The note is the staffer's own words, tidied. Nothing is added: no template, no invented time, event or outcome, no objective or intervention chosen for them. What the words do not say becomes a question (what you did, which objective, how the student responded, how long, what progress as a count). "Observed", "monitored" and "watched" are flagged, never replaced.

Captured: contact time and units come from the saved time entries (the one time model, web/js/shared/time.js, the same entries Track Time and the phone show) under the unit bands. An unconfirmed block is listed, never counted; Confirm saves it through time.save {confirm:true}, and "the afternoon block didn't happen" voids it through time.discard (kept in the record with its reason). A stopped timer nobody described is a draft, not a block. A school update is the counselor's report, credited, and the staffer decides whether it stays. The draft, each decision and the version history are kept in doc_versions.

Flows: say it or type it -> questions -> draft -> review -> Approve and fill -> Process Form (observed) -> the note is recorded with the session id and Harvest #.

## 26 · Incident report (`ExtIncident.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · the staffer's words plus the school and the signed-in person. The time, level, actions, CPS and injury are left for the person. The person opens the state portal and files it; the time they pressed it is logged.

## 32 · Quarterly report (`ExtQuarterly.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · goal progress is COUNTED from the stored notes of the student (never a constant); with no notes stored the box prints "Evidence missing - verify". The family version goes to the parent portal for the guardian's signature. The internal quarterly is staff only: it is never typed into Lauris, never on the parent portal and never in a request.

## 33 · Prepare authorization · continued stay (`ExtCase.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · the evidence-first request. The index is built from Psyflo's own captured copies of processed notes (state.notes; the M.R. corpus is seeded for Aug 18 to Oct 28: 40 daily notes whose units add up to the 83 of 110 in the authorization, 9 weekly guardian calls, 3 monthly collateral notes). Pick a period; the counts (daily, weekly, monthly) open their rows. Exclude a row or mark it sensitive: Psyflo keeps who did it and when; the note itself is never changed. Household context is NOT included until a person includes it, and only from a stored note that says it. Statements carry source chips; a missing fact prints "Evidence missing - verify ...". The panel never states the "61 notes" figure: Aug 18 to Oct 29 has 53 school days.

Flows: Open the evidence (Scan & Enhance, Evidence review, read-only) -> mark rows -> back to the request -> Review the statements -> Approve and fill -> Process Form (observed).
