## 29 · Authorizations (`Authorizations.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · one row per request, read from request.stage. Nothing on this screen changes a request: a row opens its case.

Captured: stage, units, sent date and decision day come from recorded people and observed page events (Process Form and Get Signature from the page bridge to the open Lauris page; Send from a person; a payer decision from a person with its document reference). Owner and backup owner are seeded assignments (change them in the case). Every deadline shows its source: Officially verified, Payer-specific, Harvest-reported or Unverified demo assumption; "Day N" runs from the day the request was sent, with the day-10 follow-up call and the overdue limit read from the rules table (both Harvest-reported). Units left and "school days" use the demo rate of 3 units a day (Unverified). Renewal due uses the lead time in the rules table (14 business days, Unverified) or a units balance that runs out first. The chips overlap (they are filters, not buckets). Partial, information-requested, peer-to-peer, denied and appeal rows link to Denial Intelligence. The demo controls (receive a reply, advance a day) live in the presenter dock, not here.

Flows: Process Form (observed) -> Pending clinical approval -> Approve -> Get Signature (observed) -> Signed -> Send -> Submitted -> decision recorded -> Active -> task "Enter units in Lauris" -> a person enters them -> the open Lauris page shows them -> task closed.

## 52 · Authorization case (`Authorization.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · the same request, one case. The next step is done by a person who holds the capability; Psyflo drafts and records.

Captured: Review packet and Process Form are done in Lauris by a person and recorded when the open page shows them. Approve is recorded by a designated approver (capability approve.sra) with name and time; approving is not signing. Sign in Lauris is observed: the signer's name and credential are read from the Lauris signature event, and a signer who is not on the LMHP-type roster puts the request on hold (Send stays blocked). Send needs the send capability, which can belong to someone else (a site supervisor, when designated); fax goes through the Sfax stub (Simulated) and the reference exists only after the stub returns it; a portal payer opens the portal in the clinician's own login and the open page shows the confirmation. Follow-up calls are typed by a person (Psyflo does not dial). A payer decision is recorded with its document reference and is write-once (a correction needs a reason). An approved decision puts the award in Psyflo's tracker and opens the task "Enter units in Lauris": Psyflo never writes units into Lauris, and the task closes only when the open Lauris page shows them. The timeline lists the audit trail, latest first; each event opens What, When, Who, Document, Source and Next action; a missing field reads "Not recorded". Evidence counts are computed from stored notes; missing evidence prints "Evidence missing — verify …"; admin sees counts and status but no clinical text. Transition dates are proposed from the units and the rules table with every assumption shown; a clinician sets the plan.

Flows: draft -> Process Form -> Approve -> Get Signature -> Send -> Submitted -> Log call (day 10) -> Record decision -> Active -> Enter units in Lauris.

## 19 · Sent · a person presses Send (`Submission.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · the Sent screen is now the Submission history section of the authorization case (`#/Authorization?id=…&section=submission`); the old route still opens it with legacy=1.

Captured: what was sent, how, the reference (only from the Sfax stub or the open portal page), the receipt PDF (Simulated), the receipt call, calls logged, re-sends with their reasons and the overdue notice. A re-send never resets the decision clock.

Flows: Send -> receipt -> follow-up call -> decision recorded.

## 36 · Early discharge (`EarlyDischarge.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · early discharge is the Transition section of the authorization case (`#/Authorization?id=…&section=transition`).

Captured: run-out, discharge, intake and new-initial dates are computed from the awarded units, the demo rate and the rules table (the 30-day gap is Harvest practice, Unverified; whether it is a DMAS rule is not verified). A clinician reviews and sets the plan; setting it adds four reminders. Where the case sits in Denial Intelligence, the section links there.

Flows: partial accepted -> plan proposed -> clinician sets the plan -> reminders -> discharge -> new intake -> new initial.

## 6 · Referrals (`Referrals.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · referrals come from the school portal and from a person at Harvest; the stage follows what has been recorded.

Captured: the school's form, the family link, the eligibility record, the booked screening and the intake. The per-school counts are computed from the list; the long view is Analytics.

Flows: school submits -> family link -> eligibility -> screening call -> intake.

## 7 · Referral review (`Referral.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · the school's referral form, the guardian's family link answers and what a person records.

Captured: the school form and its attachments; the guardian's answers on the family link (the text message is a stub: Simulated, nothing is sent); the eligibility record (see Eligibility check); consent and the 30-day clock for the initial request, read from the rules table (the start event and the 30 days are Harvest-reported; the consent signature mode is a configurable policy that Harvest has not validated). Eligibility is never checked from the school's form alone.

Flows: school submits -> send the family link -> guardian submits -> eligibility check -> book the screening call or route to the grant program.

## 9 · Eligibility check (`Eligibility.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · five states: details received, check required, checked by an authorized user, result with date and source, follow-up scheduled. It lives in the referral (`#/Referral?id=…&panel=eligibility`).

Captured: the lookup details come from the family link. A person with eligibility access runs the DMAS lookup in their own login (the lookup page is Simulated) and types the result; Psyflo stores the result, who checked, when and a dated proof, and schedules the re-check (every 7 days, Unverified). Psyflo never logs in to DMAS.

Flows: family link submitted -> check required -> a person runs the lookup -> result recorded with proof -> re-check reminder.
