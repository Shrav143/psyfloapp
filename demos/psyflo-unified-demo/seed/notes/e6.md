## 3 · School portal (`SchoolPortal.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · portal

Captured: the counselor's referral (see Referral form) and short updates about a student in TDT ("Share an update", school.share). Attachments are added by name (referral.attach). Per the Nov 7 product call there is no teacher portal, no teacher check-ins and no view of the treatment plan.

Flows: the home has one "Harvest needs" card (missing attachments on the school's own referrals, a referral waiting on the school) above the list of the school's referrals. The list is status text only: contacting the family, screening call booked, started TDT, routed to the grant program. Never clinical content, insurance results, another school's students or outpatient students. The school user is the session (a school link or the presenter's school view); the server refuses a referral from another school, an update about another school's or an outpatient student, and any change to a referral that was already sent.

Populates: Referrals and Referral review (staff). A counselor update never goes to Lauris on its own: staff add it to that day's daily note credited to the counselor, or file it as a collateral contact (school.shareUsed), and the school sees "waiting for staff" or "added to the daily note".

Simulated: the file itself is not uploaded; only its name is recorded. The greeting follows the demo clock.

## 4 · Referral form (school) (`ReferralForm.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · form

Captured: four sections, required first: Required (guardian agreement, student name, guardian name or phone), Student and family, Concerns and supports, Attachments and contact. Nothing is pre-checked and the interventions box starts empty: every answer is the counselor's own. The guardian agreement is attested first, in the form and on the server (a referral is refused without it).

Flows: Submit goes to Psyflo (referral.create, stage needs screening); "Save and finish later" keeps a draft that only the school sees (the draft id is remembered in this browser). Nothing goes to Lauris.

Populates: Referral review, then the screening draft and the family link (guardian name and phone are filled in for the family to check, not typed twice).

## 8 · Family link (phone) (`FamilyLink.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · link

Captured: five steps, one task per screen (about you, insurance details, permission to call, best time, getting to the intake). Which questions are asked is set in Settings · family link. Name and phone are filled in from what Harvest already has, for the guardian to check.

## 11 · Parent signs (`ParentSign.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · Parent signs

Captured: the guardian's signature on one form at a time, drawn or typed. The signer recorded is the guardian behind the link, never the typed text; the typed name is drawn into the same signed PDF.

Flows: the screen follows the signature-mode setting for that document type (Settings · family and signatures, rules signature.mode.consent, release, enrollment, quarterly, appeal): Remote allowed (sign here; chip Policy not validated in the presenter overlay), In person required (what to bring and when; no digital signature offered, and the server refuses one), Pending (Harvest is still deciding; the server refuses a signature), Completed (signed record with date and signer), Ready to review (a form Harvest has not asked for yet: read only). A digital signature never replaces a signature Harvest requires in person. The form wording is demo wording; Harvest's real forms replace it. The recording sentence in the consent follows the rule recording.require_consent_record (Harvest asks first, on the call).

Populates: the consent date on the intake, the signed PDF in Psyflo, the family checklist.

## 39 · Appeal letter, guardian side (`AppealLetter.dc.html`)

HOW THE DATA GETS IN · Appeal letter

Captured: the guardian's review, wording changes and signature. Psyflo never exercises an appeal right and never sends: signing is not sending.

Flows: four states: Draft (staff are still preparing it), Ready to review (staff prepared it in Denial Intelligence; the guardian reads, edits and signs, or calls), Signed (staff will send; request.appealSign), Submitted by staff (a person pressed Send; request.appealSend). The staff side writes appeal_drafts[requestId] = { en, es, attached, attached_es, status: draft or ready }. The seeded R-0384 letter is an Illustrative sample. The date to appeal by comes from the record (notice date) with its rule chip (appeal.window_days, unverified: confirm on the letter). A parent or guardian can appeal; who else can is not verified here. The signature follows the mode for appeals (remote, in person, pending), enforced on the server.
