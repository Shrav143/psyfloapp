How to read this. Each section is a menu tab of the app, in sidebar order. Inside it, one diagram per screen or task, from the first click to where the flow ends or hands off.

The shape tells you the kind of step and the colour tells you who does it (see the sixteen shapes below). Dashed boxes that say Continues in are links: click one to jump to that diagram. Type any button label or screen name in the search box to find every diagram that uses it. Drag a diagram to move it; use Full screen for the big ones.

No diagram mentions that. Try a button label, a screen name or an agent name.

Overview

How to read the diagrams, the whole site map, and one request from referral to decision.

The sixteen shapes

The shape says what kind of step it is. The colour says who does it. Every diagram in this document uses only these.

StartWhere the flow begins: a sidebar click, a link, an event
End (done)The flow finishes normally
End (stopped)The flow stops: blocked, declined, cancelled, nothing to do
ScreenA page the person sees
Pop-upA drawer, modal, menu or panel on top of a screen
Person clicksA person's click, tap, key press or signature
Person entersA person types, picks, drops or uploads something
System stepWhat the app does: validate, calculate, update the screen
FLO / agentAutomated work by FLO or one of the agents (Reader, Scribe, ...)
DecisionA yes/no or multi-way branch; every exit is labelled
Data savedA record is created or changed (case, request, referral, mapping, rule)
DocumentA letter, brief, report, appeal, form or file produced or read
Outside partyPayer, referrer, EMR or portal: something outside Psyflo acts
WaitTime passes or the flow waits on someone
MessageA toast, banner or notification the person sees
Continues elsewhereJumps to another diagram; the label names it
Blue: a personPurple: FLO and the agentsGrey: the appOrange: outside partyAmber: decisionTeal: data savedCream: screenGreen and red: the ends
