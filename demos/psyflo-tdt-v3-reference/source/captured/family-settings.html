How the answers reach Lauris

Psyflo cannot send anything to Lauris on its own. When the screener starts the Admission Screening in Lauris, the extension types the guardian details into the form on screen; same for the CNA header. The screener checks them and presses Process Form.
Until that is switched on: the panel shows the answers and the site supervisor types them — about a minute per family.

Message text

Hi, this is Harvest Outreach Center. [School] asked us to reach out about support for your child. Could you answer a few quick questions so we can call you? [link] · Reply STOP to opt out.
Link expires in 7 days. Reminder after 2 days if not opened.
Worth asking Lauris
Lauris has a "Consumer Access" family login. If Harvest turns it on, families could enter these details in Lauris directly and Psyflo would read them there — no typing-in step at all.
