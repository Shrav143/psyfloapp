Psyflo × Community Counseling Center of Southern Nevada · how it fits

Your EHR stays the record. Psyflo does the paperwork between the chart and the plan, and a person signs and sends.

CCC of SN bills Nevada Medicaid FFS (DHCFP) and the five southern-Nevada Medicaid MCOs: SilverSummit, Anthem, Molina, CareSource and Health Plan of Nevada. Grant clients (Ryan White, SAPTA, HOPWA) need no prior authorization.

1 · Your EHR

Stays the system of record

  • Client chart, ASAM assessment, treatment plan, session notes
  • Entitlements, expiry and units-used reminders, as today
  • Billing and claims go out the way they do now
2 · Inside the EHR · the extension

Drafts from the chart

  • Intake screening Scribe
  • ASAM assessment summary, six dimensions Scribe
  • Treatment plan Scan & enhance
  • Initial PA and continued-stay request Review a request
3 · Formatted per plan

The same facts, the shape each plan wants

  • CareSource: its own PA form, ASAM ratings on page 1
  • HPN: text ready to paste in the provider portal
  • Anthem: Availity upload
  • SilverSummit, Molina: EHR printout + cover sheet by fax
4 · A person

Signs and sends

  • The clinician signs the request
  • Billing presses Send in the portal or fax
  • Psyflo never signs and never submits on its own
5 · The Psyflo app · tracking

Watches what comes back

  • Decision clocks and information requests
  • 5-session renewals drafted at session 3
  • Denials, reconsiderations, peer-to-peer
  • Timely-filing clock per claim
  • Payer rules: plan policy changes learned from denials
