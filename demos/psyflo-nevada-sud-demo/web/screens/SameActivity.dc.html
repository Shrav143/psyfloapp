Same activity. Two individuals. Two different notes.

Both were in the same eight-hour community habilitation block, with the same staff, doing the same build. Psyflo drafts each note against that person's own plan, so the notes do not converge.

Why they differ

  • Different plans, different notes. Psyflo drafts against the individual's own goals and objectives. The activity is shared; the plan is not.
  • Counts, not adjectives. Three of four stages. Four redirections. Second of four. A reviewer can check a count against the rest of the chart.
  • It records failure too. J.T.'s note says the threshold was exceeded and no independent return was observed. A note that only ever reports progress is the one that reads as generated.
  • Your clinician signs every note. Nothing reaches the record without a person reading it first.
