## What the demo is and who uses it

Synthetic-data demo of Psyflo for Harvest Outreach Center, a Virginia school-based behavioral health provider (Therapeutic Day Treatment, TDT, billed H2016). Users are experts: clinicians, supervisors, direct-care staff, admins. Show them a working product with almost no explanatory text. Guardian pages are the exception (plain short labels). Lauris Online is Harvest's EHR; there is no integration, the Lauris panel only reads and types into the page the user has open. A person presses Process Form, Get Signature and Send. "Demo data · nothing is real" stays on every app screen.

## Research decisions that affect you

- The public standard is 7 calendar days (72 hours expedited) from Jan 1 2026; payers can extend; Harvest believes 14 and calls the payer around day 10 (faxes go astray). The app shows `Day N`, `Day 10 · follow-up`, `Overdue` only after 14 days. Never state the 7-day rule as a Harvest rule.
- The treatment-plan "24 hours" is Harvest's practice, not a verified Medicaid rule; do not present it as one. The daily minimum (one hour vs two hours) is unresolved: show neither.
- Nov 7 priorities: referral → initial SRA and the data flow behind it come first; compliance, analytics and payer-rule screens are "not a priority", so keep Documentation, Analytics and Payer rules light and short. Never edit notes already saved in Lauris ("scan and enhance" of saved notes was removed over audit risk); the panel drafts only into a form that is open.
- TDT unit bands: 1 unit 2–2.99 h, 2 units 3–4.99 h, 3 units 5 h or more (12VAC30-80-30). The redesign (15-minute units, CPST) starts July 1 2027.
- Synthetic numbers carry a `Sample data` tag, never "Harvest's export".
